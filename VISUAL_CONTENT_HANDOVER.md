## Asset 3 — YouTube / Loom Demo Video (Full Walkthrough)

**Duration:** 5–8 minutes
**Format:** 16:9, 1080p
**Style:** Screen recording with voiceover. Talking head intro/outro optional.

### Script outline

---

**INTRO (0:00–0:45)**

> "Hey — if you've ever tried to design a composite part without access to Fibersim or CATIA, you know how hard it is to find reliable information.
>
> Most of what exists is either textbook-dense, paywalled, or buried in vendor documentation.
>
> Today I'm walking through OpenComposites — a free, open-source platform we built to solve exactly that problem. I'll show you the AI part analysis, the knowledge base, and the engineering calculators. Takes about 5 minutes to understand what it can do for you."

---

**SECTION 1: Homepage tour (0:45–1:30)**

> Navigate to opencomposites.addcomposites.com
> "The homepage gives you access to four areas — Analyze a Part, the Knowledge Base, the Materials Browser, and the Engineering Calculators. No login, no account, nothing."
>
> Point out the stats: 56 articles, 7 calculators, 40+ materials.

---

**SECTION 2: AI part analysis demo (1:30–3:30)**

> Navigate to Analyze page
> "Let's say I'm designing a drone arm — 200mm long, carbon fibre, needs to handle about 50 newtons of lateral load, and I want it as light as possible."
>
> Type in the description. Show the API key field.
> "The AI runs on Claude — Anthropic's model. You bring your own API key, which means you're not trusting us with your account. Takes 30 seconds to set up at console.anthropic.com if you don't have one."
>
> Submit. Walk through results:
> - Material recommendation and why
> - Fibre orientation logic
> - Manufacturing process recommendation
> - Cost estimate
>
> "Notice it explains the reasoning — it's not just giving you a number, it's giving you the engineering logic behind it."

---

**SECTION 3: Knowledge base (3:30–5:00)**

> Navigate to Knowledge Base
> "The knowledge base is 56 articles. Let me search for something specific — ply drop-offs."
>
> Search, show results, click an article.
> "It's written in plain English. No textbook walls. Each section is short enough to read in 2 minutes. And it's CC BY 4.0 — that means you can use it in your own projects, your thesis, your product documentation."
>
> Show one or two more topics — stacking sequences, failure criteria.

---

**SECTION 4: Engineering calculators (5:00–6:30)**

> Navigate to a calculator (CLT or sandwich panel)
> "Beyond the AI, there are proper engineering calculators. The CLT calculator does ABD matrices, effective laminate moduli, and failure analysis. Let me put in a simple layup..."
>
> Enter a [0/45/-45/90]s layup, carbon/epoxy. Run the calculation.
> "You get the full ABD stiffness matrix, effective Ex, Ey, Gxy, and failure margins against Tsai-Wu and max stress."
>
> "There's also a sandwich panel analyser for foam and honeycomb core designs, and a bolted joint tool for bearing and bypass analysis."

---

**OUTRO (6:30–7:30)**

> "Everything is free. The app, the knowledge base, the calculators — no paywall, no account.
>
> The knowledge base is also on GitHub — CC BY 4.0 — so if you find an error, you can open a PR. If there's a topic missing, you can contribute an article.
>
> Link is in the description. If you found this useful, share it with whoever on your team is doing composites work."
